Install HousePlan on your phone

You can add the full planner to your home screen without using an app store.

  1. Open this website in Chrome on Android.
  2. Tap the browser's ⋮ menu.
  3. Choose Install app or Add to Home screen, then confirm.

Once installed, open it from your app drawer. The editor works offline after its first successful download.
